What do the core screens look like?

Phone-first screens for split-a-bill-equally. Click through the flow inside the phone, or jump to any screen from the list. Three variants of the group page and the add-expense step switch from the bar at the bottom (or the ← → keys). Sign-in, email, invite and home screens are the same in every variant.

    1. Group page: which variant, or which parts of each?
    2. Balance wording: A says "owes / is owed / even", B shows signed amounts, C says "owes / gets back / is square".
    3. Adding an expense: a full page (A), a bottom sheet (B), or a composer under the ledger (C)?
    4. Invite link: after the first friend joins, it moves down (A), into People (B), or into the group's info (C). Is that findable enough?
    5. Empty states: one member puts the invite link first. No expenses shows one line. Right?
    6. Description: required here, up to 100 characters. Group name up to 50.
    7. Sign-in email: open it from "Check your email". Are the sender, subject and wording right, including the join version?
    8. Confirm page: shows the email address being signed in, and the group being joined.
